    Step 01Film study

    Read the data before the brief.

    At EcoSmart I built call tracking myself before spending a dollar on ads.
    Step 02The call

    Pick the one lever that moves profit.

    Not clicks, not impressions. Cost per lead, profit per lead, and what a customer is worth.
